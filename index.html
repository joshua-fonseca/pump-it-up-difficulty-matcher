What is PIU Song Matcher?

PIU Song Matcher helps two Pump It Up players find songs they can comfortably play together. Set each player's minimum and maximum difficulty, then browse matching charts across Pump It Up versions.

How does it work?

Choose each player's comfortable difficulty range and filter songs by Arcade, Remix, Shortcut, Full Song, or Pump It Up version. Matching charts are highlighted for each player.
